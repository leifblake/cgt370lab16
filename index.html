RuPaul’s Drag Race Network Visualization

LB | CGT 370 | Lab 16

Welcome to the RuPaul’s Drag Race Network Visualization. This interactive network displays the connections between contestants (queens) and the seasons of RuPaul’s Drag Race, including both the main series and All Stars seasons.

Each dot represents either a queen or a season. Queens are color-coded based on whether they appear only once or in multiple seasons, while season nodes are distinguished by the series they belong to. Red lines between a queen and their season indicate that they won that season. Hover over any node to see more details, including the specific seasons a queen appeared in or the season details.
